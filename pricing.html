Pricing

Transparent hourly pricing for independent marketing measurement, modelling, audits, advisory work and training.

Standard consulting rate

£75 per hour

All JTP Analytics work is based on an hourly rate of £75. The total project price depends on complexity: the question being answered, the number and quality of data sources, the scale of the model, the validation required and the level of documentation or delivery support.

The aim is to keep pricing transparent while avoiding a fixed package that charges a simple project as though it were a complex one.

What you get with JTP Analytics

The hourly rate covers more than the technical service itself. You work directly with the person delivering the analysis, receive practical advice throughout the engagement and retain visibility into how the work was produced.

This is an example rather than a fixed package. A simpler or more complex project is priced from the same £75 hourly rate. Additional outcomes, channels, markets, data preparation, bespoke model components, reporting and training change the time required.

What affects the scope?

  • Data readiness: whether the required data is already clean, aligned and documented.
  • Model scale: the number of outcomes, channels, campaigns, products, regions or customer groups.
  • Method: whether the project uses a standard structure or requires bespoke statistical development.
  • Validation: the amount of holdout testing, sensitivity analysis and experimental calibration required.
  • Outputs: whether you need a focused answer, reusable code, a written report, decision tools or stakeholder presentations.
  • Handover: the level of documentation, training and ongoing support your team needs.

How a quote is prepared

  1. You send a short description of the decision, available data and desired timeline.
  2. JTP holds an initial conversation to clarify the problem and assess fit.
  3. You receive a proposed scope with an estimated number of hours and expected deliverables.
  4. Any meaningful change in scope is discussed before additional work is undertaken.

Short advisory calls, training, model reviews and agency support use the same £75 hourly rate. Larger projects are also built from that rate, with the expected time agreed during scoping.

Limited concurrent availability

JTP deliberately works with no more than four clients at once. This keeps the work senior-led and ensures each project receives proper attention rather than being passed through a large delivery team.
